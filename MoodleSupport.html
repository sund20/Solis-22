System Setup

Before logging into Moodle, you will need to make sure you have a compatible browser. Recommended browsers when using Moodle include:

  • Google Chrome
  • Firefox

Other Applications

In order to view some files, media or other items that may be available in your course, you may need some of the following software:

  • Adobe Flash
  • Windows Media Player
  • Java
  • Adobe Reader

All Moodle courses reside in MyClass (myclass.norquest.ca) If your course has a Moodle component, you will receive an email to your MyMail account which contains your username and password. Your username and password are usually:

Username: firstinitial+lastname+3 distinct numbers (eg: jsmith123)

This is the same username you use for MyQuest and MyMail.

Password: norquest+last 4 digits of your student id number
(eg. norquest4321)

If you have difficulty logging into Moodle:

  • please contact Computer Commons at 780.644.6085 or computercommons@norquest.ca.
  • You can also access the Student Manual (PDF) which details these login procedures as well as other Moodle tips.
  • If you wish to receive further training or help with your Moodle courses, book an appointment at http://libguides.norquest.ca/moodlehelp.

We can help you at the Edmonton Downtown campus 5th floor Learning Center, online or over the phone.

Good luck with your studies and Happy Moodling!
